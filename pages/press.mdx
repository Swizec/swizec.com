### Short bio

Swizec Teller is a software engineer, author, and conference speaker. He builds software at Plasmidsaurus and writes about the work behind shipping in hypergrowth startups: technical leadership, growing teams, making systems useful. His books include *Scaling Fast*, *Senior Engineer Mindset*, and *Serverless Handbook*.

### Longer bio

Swizec Teller is a software engineer, author, and conference speaker with two decades of experience building for the web. His work has taken him through startups in education, healthcare, and biotech; he now builds software at Plasmidsaurus. He writes about the practical side of engineering: scaling teams and systems, navigating technical debt, growing into leadership, and using AI while staying responsible for what ships. He is the author of *Scaling Fast: Software Engineering Through the Hockeystick*, *Senior Engineer Mindset*, and *Serverless Handbook*. Through his writing, conference talks, and workshops, Swizec helps engineers connect technical decisions to business and user outcomes. Find his work at swizec.com.

## Interview and speaking topics

- **Engineering in the age of AI.** What changes when code gets cheap, how teams use coding agents, and why judgment and production ownership matter more than ever.
- **Scaling fast.** Growing the business, the team, and the technology together; balancing speed, quality, and technical debt during startup growth.
- **The senior engineer mindset.** Earning trust, taking ownership, mentoring, and leading beyond the code.
- **Product engineering.** Learning unfamiliar domains, understanding users' workflows, and measuring whether software actually helps.
- **Modern web development.** React, TypeScript, serverless backends, data visualization, and incremental rewrites of production systems.
