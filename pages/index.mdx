# Software Engineering Lessons from Production

You want to ship useful software, make better technical decisions, and help your team move faster. Here you'll find lessons for the messy parts: changing requirements, growing codebases, and a business that needs results.

Hi, I'm Swizec 👋 I write about shipping in hypergrowth startups: technical leadership, growing teams, and making systems useful.

**Build the judgment to take on bigger problems.** Learn when to rewrite, which technical debt to live with, how to delegate decisions, and what to check when AI writes the code.

![Swizec speaking at React Summit in Amsterdam](./img/react-summit-close-2.jpeg)

You're learning from two decades of building and teaching software. I've [rewritten a live app while the team kept shipping](/blog/how-to-rewrite-your-app-while-growing-to-a-dollar100000000-series-b/), taught engineers from Netflix, Uber, and 23andMe in my [data visualization workshops](/workshops#react-for-data-visualization), and written [Scaling Fast](https://scalingfastbook.com) and [Senior Engineer Mindset](/senior-mindset). You get the decisions, the tradeoffs, and what happened next, so you can apply the lessons to your own work.

## The engineer behind the writing

I'm a software engineer, author, and conference speaker. My career has included a failed startup, production rewrites, and learning to lead as a team grew around me. Plenty of chances to discover that my clever idea wasn't going to survive contact with users 😅

I keep doing the engineering work because that's where the next useful lesson comes from. The books, articles, and workshops are how I make those lessons useful to you.
