# Software engineering lessons from production

Hi, I'm Swizec 👋 I build software at Plasmidsaurus and write about shipping in hypergrowth startups: technical leadership, growing teams, and making systems useful.

I'm the author of [Scaling Fast](https://scalingfastbook.com), [Senior Engineer Mindset](/senior-mindset), and [Serverless Handbook](https://serverlesshandbook.dev). Here I share what I'm learning from the work, after two decades of building for the web. Without the fluff.

![Swizec speaking at React Summit in Amsterdam](./img/react-summit-close-2.jpeg)

Want to ship better software, take on bigger problems, and lead a team through the messy parts? That's what I write about. The code, the tradeoffs, and the times I got it wrong.
